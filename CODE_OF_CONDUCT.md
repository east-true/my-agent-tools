# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in this project, regardless of background, identity, experience, or preferred language. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, and other project spaces, including when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate. Give people time to respond and room to make mistakes.
- Discuss the work and explain technical disagreements with concrete examples.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for the impact of your words and actions.
- Respect privacy. Obtain permission before publishing another person's contact details or private communications.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual advances, personal attacks, doxxing, and repeated disruption are unacceptable. Disagreement about an implementation or project decision is welcome when expressed respectfully.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, check the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/east-true) for available contact options. Include links or other relevant context and describe what happened. English and Korean are welcome. Do not publish sensitive details in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports and limit disclosure to what is necessary to investigate and respond. Reporters may describe their preferred follow-up or confidentiality needs. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is unacceptable.

For abuse that requires action by GitHub, or a concern involving the maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Report software vulnerabilities through the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, give a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a participant, depending on the severity and recurrence of the behavior. When appropriate, the maintainer will explain the decision privately. Participants may request a review by replying to the maintainer with additional context.
